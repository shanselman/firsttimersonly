# 🌟 Welcome! Let's Do Some Open Source Together

Contributing to open source for the first time can be a little intimidating — but we’re here to make it **friendly, approachable, and fun**. Whether you’re a [Code Newbie](https://www.codenewbie.org) or a seasoned developer trying to give back, you’re in the right place.

---

## 🚀 You Can Do It — Here’s How to Start

If you’re just getting started, these beginner-friendly resources will guide you through your first contribution:

- 🧪 [**First Contributions**](https://github.com/firstcontributions/first-contributions#first-contributions)  
  Hands-on tutorial walking you through a complete contribution on GitHub.
  
- 🏷 [**Up for Grabs**](https://up-for-grabs.net)  
  Curated list of projects that are actively looking for contributors with "help wanted" or "good first issue" labels.
  
- 🧰 [**goodfirstissues.com**](https://goodfirstissues.com)  
  A real-time list of easy-to-tackle issues across GitHub.
  
- 🔍 [**goodfirstissue.dev**](https://goodfirstissue.dev/)  
  Curated "easy picks" from top repositories to help you get started quickly.
  
- 📬 [**CodeTriage**](https://www.codetriage.com/)  
  Subscribe to open-source projects and get new open issues in your inbox daily.

- 📚 [**Contribute Guide by Hanselman**](https://www.hanselman.com/blog/get-involved-in-open-source-today-how-to-contribute-a-patch-to-a-github-hosted-open-source-project-like-code-52)  
  A friendly, detailed guide on making your first patch.

- 🎬 [**Get Involved in Tech**](https://getinvolved.hanselman.com/)  
  A documentary training film on getting started with GitHub, StackOverflow, blogging, and more.

- 🔍 [**First Pull Request**](https://firstpr.me/)  
  See what someone else's first PR looked like (they're usually small!).

## 🛠 Are You a Maintainer?

You can help new contributors by labeling a few issues with:

### `first-timers-only`

This means:
> *"I'm willing to help you through this PR. It’s beginner-friendly. If you’re new, this one’s for you!"*
